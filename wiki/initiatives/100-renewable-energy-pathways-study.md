The 100% Renewable Energy Pathways Study is a technical study commissioned by OSI outlining pathways for Ann Arbor to achieve 100% renewable electricity powering the entire community. OSI shared the study's results with the public in Year Four, part of a broader Strategy 1 push that also included community choice aggregation legislation, the [[initiatives/sustainable-energy-utility|Sustainable Energy Utility]] rate analysis, and the [[initiatives/municipalization-study-phase-ii|Phase II Municipalization Study]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
