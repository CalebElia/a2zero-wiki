The 2030 Carbon Neutral Ann Arbor Plan, known as A2Zero, was initiated by a November 4, 2019 City Council resolution that declared a climate emergency and directed the City Administration to develop a community-wide carbon neutrality plan ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The resolution called for a public engagement process beginning immediately to outline how the entire Ann Arbor community could achieve carbon neutrality by 2030, described as 'both ambitious and achievable' ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|Office of Sustainability and Innovations]], created in FY19, was the lead office responsible for developing the plan, with funding for public engagement available in the FY2020 OSI budget ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The plan built on Ann Arbor's prior 2012 Climate Action Plan, which had set GHG reduction targets of 8% by 2015, 25% by 2025, and 90% by 2050 relative to a year 2000 baseline ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

A2Zero is explicitly designed as a "living plan": each Annual Report (Years 1–5) functions as a formal, structured self-review — assessing what's working, what needs adjustment, and what to add — rather than a simple progress bulletin ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year1|a2zero-year1]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]).

In November 2022, Ann Arbor voters approved the 20-year Community Climate Action Millage, giving the plan a dedicated public funding mechanism and making Ann Arbor the 8th community in the United States to tax itself specifically to fund local climate action ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]; see [[political-events/2022-11-community-climate-action-millage]]).

The plan has been progressively embedded into standard City governance rather than operating alongside it: an A2Zero-derived Sustainability Review became part of the City's plan review process for new construction ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]; see [[initiatives/sustainability-review-plan-process]]), A2Zero was integrated into the Unified Development Code ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]), and the City has worked to embed A2Zero into its Comprehensive Plan update ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).

Progress against the plan's 2030 target is tracked through the same annual community-wide GHG inventory that informed its original design ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]) — see [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations]] and [[locations/ann-arbor]] for the measured results.
