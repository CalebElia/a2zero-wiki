The [[initiatives/ann-arbor-sustainable-food-framework|Ann Arbor Sustainable Food Framework]] is the city's policy-development and formal food-policy effort within A2Zero's materials and waste strategy, intended to advance more sustainable food options at city-operated facilities and events. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The initiative aims to develop a citywide approach to sustainable food systems in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) Earlier reporting described a drafted sustainable food framework and local food programming as part of the city's broader food-system engagement, and a Year 3 priority was to create the framework and pilot it at City facilities. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) A rough draft, developed with a working group of local business owners and City staff, was already underway by Year Three ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). The framework extends Strategy 5's food-systems work beyond public engagement into municipal operating practices. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In Year Five, OSI reported completing the first draft of the framework. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) Year 6 priorities say the city will launch a sustainable food strategy. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
