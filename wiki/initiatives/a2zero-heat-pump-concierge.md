The [[initiatives/a2zero-heat-pump-concierge|A2ZERO Heat Pump Concierge]] is a resident-facing electrification service launched by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] to help Ann Arbor households replace old furnaces with heat pumps. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program provides an easy platform for residents to obtain system design and pricing estimates, including applicable rebates, along with contractor and installation oversight. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) It implements Strategy 2 by reducing practical barriers to household heating electrification. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
