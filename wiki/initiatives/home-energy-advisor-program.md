The A2ZERO Home Energy Advisor Program is a resident-facing pilot service designed and launched in Year Three of A2ZERO in partnership with [[actors/elevate|Elevate]] and [[actors/ecoworks|EcoWorks]] to assist residents with home electrification and broader household decarbonization planning ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). The service provides free home energy or needs assessments, customized home decarbonization plans, technical advice, contractor engagement, incentives and rebates guidance, bid reviews, and connections to resources for making building-efficiency upgrades and other improvements that reduce greenhouse gas emissions in residents’ homes ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). The program is considered foundational work intended to create scalable new initiatives in Year Four that will help residents and businesses transition to cleaner and healthier ways to heat and power their lives ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). In Year Five, the program launched and helped over 300 Ann Arbor residents obtain these assessments and plans ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
