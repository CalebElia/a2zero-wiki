A dedicated funding source approved by Ann Arbor voters for local climate action programs. Cited in the Year 3 annual report as enabling residential rebates and other A2Zero implementation work ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]).

The Community Climate Action Millage is Ann Arbor's dedicated local climate funding mechanism ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The Year 5 priorities call for continued grant fundraising with a goal of achieving a 1:1 match to the millage ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
