The [[initiatives/move-out-ann-arbor-coalition|Move Out Ann Arbor Coalition]] was newly formed to collect unwanted materials for reuse during the University of Michigan student move-out period in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In its inaugural year, the coalition included representatives from the [[actors/university-of-michigan|University of Michigan]], local nonprofits, haulers, and the city, and it diverted approximately 34 tons of material from local landfills during the recent student move-out season. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
