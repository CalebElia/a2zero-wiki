The Ann Arbor Climate Corps was launched in year four as a national AmeriCorps program that supports education and behavior change related to creating a more sustainable future in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The program is described as one of the city's "other actions" that advances A2ZERO through engagement-oriented work spanning multiple strategies. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) It is also described as an AmeriCorps-backed implementation program supporting [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] with sustainability education, outreach, and program delivery in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In year five, 14 AmeriCorps members served with the program, 8 completed full terms, and they collectively served 13,600 hours with OSI. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) Those members earned Segal Education Awards totaling $55,109, and the city expected four members to complete second terms and six members to complete first terms in the next fiscal year. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
