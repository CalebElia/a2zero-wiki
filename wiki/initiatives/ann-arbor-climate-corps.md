The Ann Arbor Climate Corps was launched in year four as a national AmeriCorps program that supports education and behavior change related to creating a more sustainable future in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The program is described as one of the city's "other actions" that advances A2ZERO through engagement-oriented work spanning multiple strategies. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
