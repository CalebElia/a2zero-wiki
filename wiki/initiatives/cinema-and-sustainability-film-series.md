[[initiatives/cinema-and-sustainability-film-series|Cinema and Sustainability Film Series]] is a public engagement program sponsored and hosted by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] at the [[locations/michigan-theater|Michigan Theater]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In 2024, the series screened four documentaries with post-film panel discussions on food waste, extreme heat, renewable energy, factory farming, and environmental justice, drawing more than 250 attendees across all screenings. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) OSI reported plans to repeat the series in 2025. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
