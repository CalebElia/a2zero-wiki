The Energy Concierge and Community Engagement Program creates an easy-to-use service to help residents and businesses understand the best, highest-impact, and most affordable methods to carry out greenhouse gas reduction activities ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The program is described as foundational to the entire carbon neutrality effort, targeting all Ann Arborites, and is intended to continue operating even after carbon neutrality is achieved ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The 10-year cost is estimated at $820,000; the initiative does not directly reduce GHG emissions but enables residents and businesses to find, access, and implement efficiency, renewable energy, and electrification activities outlined in the plan ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). By 2025, the program aims to have helped 2,500 Ann Arborites access energy-related programs and support resources ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). CAP-2020 separately floated a joint county/city framing for the same concept, proposing coordinated guidance across both city and county jurisdictions to accelerate adoption of efficiency upgrades and renewables ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

As of Year Two, the City was co-designing the service with community partners as a one-on-one advisory program to help residents develop prescriptive maps toward home improvements and decarbonization, with testing planned for Year Three ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). No annual report through Year Five confirms this specific co-designed pilot actually launched under this name — the concept's real-world operationalization instead took the form of a separate, differently-branded service (below).

In Year Four, the A2ZERO Home Energy Advisor service launched as a free service available to anyone in the City of Ann Arbor to help residents determine their next best step on the path to decarbonization ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). This service operationalizes the CAP's energy concierge concept by providing resident-facing guidance within Ann Arbor's broader building efficiency and electrification work ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]); see [[initiatives/home-energy-advisor-program]] for its own launch history and results. A narrower, later spin-off focused specifically on heat pump replacement guidance launched in Year Five as the [[initiatives/a2zero-heat-pump-concierge|A2ZERO Heat Pump Concierge]].
