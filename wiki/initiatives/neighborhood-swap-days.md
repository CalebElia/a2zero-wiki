Neighborhood Swap Days are recurring reuse-focused community events supported by OSI to keep usable goods circulating within the community instead of being landfilled, implementing Strategy 5's emphasis on reuse, repair, and repurposing before disposal. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program began in Year Three, when the City hosted three neighborhood swap days and created a replication guide so others could host swap days in their own neighborhoods or organizations ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). The program continued in Year Four under Strategy 5, when the City reported collaborating with [[actors/community-action-network|Community Action Network]], Church of the Good Shepherd, [[actors/ann-arbor-public-schools|Ann Arbor Public Schools]], and [[actors/zerowaste-org|ZeroWaste.Org]] to reuse more than 6,800 items through the program. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) In Year Five, OSI hosted five Neighborhood Swap Day events and supported additional community-led swap events, keeping more than 10,000 items in reuse. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
