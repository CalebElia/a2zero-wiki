The A2Zero plan identifies the Green Business Challenge as an existing program to be accelerated and scaled as part of community engagement with the commercial and institutional sector on resource reduction ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The challenge is listed alongside a commercial reuse packaging program, engagement with the private sector, and requirements or encouragement for disclosure from businesses and community groups ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

The [[initiatives/green-business-challenge|Ann Arbor Green Business Challenge]] is a business-facing, commercial-sector sustainability and climate initiative under A2Zero, launched in 2024 by OSI in partnership with [[actors/ann-arbor-spark|SPARK]] to recognize, support, and celebrate local businesses advancing sustainability in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). The program was designed in collaboration with [[actors/spark-ann-arbor|SPARK]] and local businesses, with design work beginning in Year 3 of A2Zero, to encourage sustainable business practices in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). It launched in Year Four in partnership with [[actors/ann-arbor-spark|SPARK]], is open to anyone in the City, and promotes, recognizes, and supports local businesses in improving the sustainability of their operations ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).

The challenge is part of Ann Arbor's building-efficiency implementation stack for businesses and complements benchmarking, reporting, and on-demand decarbonization assistance; it is also supported through broader commercial-building assistance partnerships including the [[actors/ann-arbor-washtenaw-2030-district|Ann Arbor / Washtenaw 2030 District]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).

In Year Five, the program welcomed [[actors/fresh-coast-climate-solutions|Fresh Coast Climate Solutions]] to provide individualized work plans for participating businesses and hired four interns to assist with programming and outreach aimed at bringing more businesses into the challenge ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). A stated scaling goal is to reach 50 local businesses, identified in Year 5 priorities and again in Year 6 priorities ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
