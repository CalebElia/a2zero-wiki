The Tree Canopy initiative is projected to reduce 450 MT CO2e at a total cost of $1,160,000 and approximately $2,578 per ton ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). Co-benefits include air quality improvement, resilience, public health, biodiversity preservation, equity benefits, and scalability ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

In Year Three, the City prepared and submitted a grant proposal to the USDA Forest Service to fund expanded tree canopy work ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). That effort paid off in Year Four, when Ann Arbor won a $1,000,000 grant from the USDA Forest Service to fund tree canopy analysis, tree planting, and stewardship of the [[locations/ann-arbor|Natural Area Preserves (NAP)]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In Year Five, the City secured a separate $1,000,000 grant from American Forests specifically targeted at advancing tree equity across the community ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
