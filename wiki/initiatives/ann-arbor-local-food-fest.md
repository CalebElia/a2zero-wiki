The Ann Arbor Local Food Fest is a recurring community event that celebrates the local food system and connects residents with farmers, chefs, and community organizations. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In Year Five, the city held the 3rd Annual Ann Arbor Local Food Fest in collaboration with [[locations/ann-arbor-farmers-market|the Ann Arbor Farmers Market]] and [[actors/argus-farm-stop|Argus Farm Stop]], drawing hundreds of participants. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report also indicated that another Local Food Fest was planned for 2025. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
