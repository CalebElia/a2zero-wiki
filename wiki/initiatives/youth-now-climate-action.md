[[initiatives/youth-now-climate-action|Youth Now Climate Action]] is a youth engagement initiative created by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] in collaboration with [[actors/leslie-science-and-nature-center|Leslie Science and Nature Center]] and [[actors/neutral-zone|Neutral Zone]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program formed a small group of high school students representing various [[actors/ann-arbor-public-schools|Ann Arbor Public Schools]] who focus on highlighting environmental problems and developing solutions.
