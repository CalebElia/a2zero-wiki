In year five, the City launched Borrow a Cargo Bike A2 in collaboration with [[actors/human-electric-hybrids|Human Electric Hybrids]] and [[actors/bicycle-alliance-of-washtenaw|Bicycle Alliance of Washtenaw]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program invites residents to sign up and try a cargo bike, positioning it as a practical non-car mobility option within Strategy 4. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report presents the program as part of Ann Arbor's effort to reduce vehicle miles traveled by supporting more biking and other non-motorized travel. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
