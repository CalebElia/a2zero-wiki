OSI began airing Green Light, a recurring show on CTN featuring residents who are envisioning, creating, and establishing a sustainable and equitable future for the community. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) Green Light Program is a short interview program produced by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] on Ann Arbor's Community Television Network. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) OSI also began airing a recurring sustainability segment on local radio station 107.1 to consistently promote timely events and announcements. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) A year-four Green Light episode focused on the [[initiatives/home-energy-rating-disclosure-program|Home Energy Rating Disclosure]] ordinance and was hosted by Grace Franke with energy assessor guests Jerrell Wylie and Steve Christensen. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) In year five, OSI reported filming nine episodes covering topics including electric lawn care, the [[initiatives/sustainable-energy-utility|Sustainable Energy Utility]], the [[initiatives/a2zero-ambassadors-program|A2ZERO Ambassador Program]], and the history of Earth Day in Ann Arbor, with a combined total of 2,000 views. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
