[[initiatives/home-energy-rebate-program|Home Energy Rebate Program]] is a city program being designed to make building electrification, building efficiency improvements, and e-bikes more affordable for Ann Arbor residents. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) In Year Four, Ann Arbor initiated a 3-year contract with [[actors/elevate|Elevate]] to design and launch the program, aimed at increasing the affordability of energy efficiency improvements, with local rebates slated to begin in summer 2024. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The City also authorized over $4,000,000 in funds to be used for residential rebates over the coming 2.5 years. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
