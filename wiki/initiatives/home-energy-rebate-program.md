[[initiatives/home-energy-rebate-program|Home Energy Rebate Program]] is Ann Arbor's city program being designed to make home energy improvements, building electrification, building efficiency improvements, and cleaner transportation options such as e-bikes more affordable for residents. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In Year Four, the city initiated a three-year contract with [[actors/elevate|Elevate]] to design and launch the program, aimed at increasing the affordability of energy efficiency improvements, and authorized more than $4,000,000 in funds for residential rebates over roughly 2.5 years, with local rebates slated to begin in summer 2024. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) By May 2025, the rebates had launched and more than 650 had been claimed for weatherization and air sealing, heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, induction stoves, heat pump dryers, batteries, electrical upgrades, and e-bikes. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
