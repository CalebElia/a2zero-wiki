The [[initiatives/electric-refuse-truck-program|Electric Refuse Truck Program]] is Ann Arbor's effort to electrify heavy-duty sanitation vehicles as part of broader municipal fleet decarbonization and supports the purchase of electric refuse trucks for Ann Arbor's municipal fleet. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) Funding of $270,000 was secured from the [[actors/american-lung-association|American Lung Association]] and $406,000 from the State of Michigan to support the purchase of two electric refuse trucks. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) In Year Five, the city purchased two more electric refuse trucks, bringing the total on order to four, and reported that the first electric refuse truck began providing service that spring. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) OSI also received grant support from the [[actors/american-lung-association|American Lung Association]] for partial funding of two recently ordered electric side-load Mack refuse trucks. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
