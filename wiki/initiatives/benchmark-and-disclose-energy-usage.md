title: Benchmark and Disclose Energy Usage
type: initiative

The Benchmark and Disclose Energy Usage initiative establishes standardized energy audits and self-reporting requirements for homeowners, landlords, businesses, and institutions to identify where the most significant efficiency improvements can be made ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The program requires building a back-end portal to automatically report energy usage between utilities and the City, with sufficient code enforcement staff to ensure compliance ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The 10-year cost is estimated at $2,370,000, and the initiative is described as foundational for enabling other energy efficiency actions in the plan rather than directly reducing greenhouse gas emissions on its own ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In Year Four, the City continued implementing the Commercial and Multi-Family Energy and Water Benchmarking Ordinance, with all buildings greater than 20,000 square feet required to track and report their energy and water usage annually, while also launching the companion [[initiatives/home-energy-rating-disclosure-program|Home Energy Rating Disclosure]] effort ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). An equity concern noted is that lower-income homeowners may face lower home sale prices or rising rents in efficient rental units as a result of the program ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).
