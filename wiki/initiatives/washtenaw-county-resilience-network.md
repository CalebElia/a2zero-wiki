The [[initiatives/washtenaw-county-resilience-network|Washtenaw County Resilience Network]] is a countywide resilience-coordination effort co-hosted by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] and the [[actors/washtenaw-county-resilience-office|Washtenaw County Resilience Office]]. It is also the regional resilience initiative the city sought to start through a $1,000,000 U.S. EPA Environmental Justice Government-to-Government grant, with the goal of expanding [[initiatives/resilience-hubs|Resilience Hubs]] in Ann Arbor and throughout Washtenaw County. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In Year Five, the network convened critical service providers from across Washtenaw County to discuss resilience initiatives, partnership-building, and collaborative approaches to climate resilience work. The city reported that it was advancing this work, but also received a letter terminating the EPA award and said it was actively disputing that termination. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
