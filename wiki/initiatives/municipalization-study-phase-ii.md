Traditional municipalization has been described by the city as one of the parallel pathways under exploration for achieving 100% renewable electricity in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) In Year Three, OSI commissioned an Energy Options Analysis to compare the financial viability of the SEU, traditional municipalization, and other options. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]) Year Four reports that Council authorized a Phase II Municipalization Study in May 2024 and that the city launched a public comment process on the study scope. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The stated purpose of the Phase II study is to conduct a detailed valuation of [[actors/dte-energy|DTE Energy]]'s electric assets in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
