The [[initiatives/nourishing-neighborhood-pilot|Nourishing Neighborhood Pilot]] was developed in Year Four with support from three ReDirect fellows working in coordination with [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] and the [[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Office of Emergency Management]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The pilot then moved into neighborhood engagement, with A2ZERO Ambassadors leading an initial gathering in the [[locations/darlington-neighborhood|Darlington Neighborhood]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). A second pilot event focused on residents' experiences with winter power outages and discussions of emergency preparedness, indicating the program's emphasis on neighborhood-level resilience and mutual support ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
