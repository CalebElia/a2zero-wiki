The [[initiatives/more-life-less-stuff-campaign|More Life, Less Stuff Campaign]] was launched in Year Four as a social based marketing effort under Ann Arbor's circularity and waste-reduction work ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The campaign compiles stories from community partners and residents about reducing waste and engaging more with the world around them, framing reuse and lower-consumption behavior as a community practice rather than only a disposal issue ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
