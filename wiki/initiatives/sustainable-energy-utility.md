title: Sustainable Energy Utility (SEU)
type: initiative
---

The Sustainable Energy Utility (SEU) is a proposed community-owned local energy utility model under A2Zero's renewable grid strategy that would provide electricity generated from local solar and battery storage systems installed on homes and businesses throughout Ann Arbor — delivering 100% clean, reliable, locally built, and affordable electricity, described as built by the community for the community. It is one of two parallel pathways under active exploration for achieving 100% renewable electricity, alongside traditional municipalization of the electric grid. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])

A formal proposal for the SEU was developed in Year Two, and the City opened a public engagement portal at www.a2gov.org/a2seu for residents to learn more and register their input. The SEU proposal was awarded the Project of the Year by the [[actors/great-lakes-renewable-energy-association|Great Lakes Renewable Energy Association]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]])

In Year Three, [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] commissioned an Energy Options Analysis to determine the financial viability of the SEU, traditional municipalization, and other options for meeting community-wide renewable energy goals. A grant application was also submitted to the U.S. Department of Energy to formally create the SEU, with decisions expected in mid-2024. A strategy was expected to be brought to Council for formal consideration by the end of Year Four. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]])

In Year Four, the city conducted a technical study and rate analysis for the SEU and launched a community education and engagement initiative describing it as an optional, supplemental, and community-owned utility authorized to deploy renewable energy in Ann Arbor. The report states that voters will have the opportunity to decide whether the City should formally create the SEU in the November 2024 election. In the Year 5 priorities, the City identified support for education, engagement, and the potential launch of a local Ann Arbor Sustainable Energy Utility as a next-step priority. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])

The initiative is rooted in Strategy 1's goal of a 100% renewable, reliable, and affordable electric grid for Ann Arbor by 2030.
