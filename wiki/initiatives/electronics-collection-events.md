Electronics Collection Events are a new OSI service offering created with [[actors/comprenew|Comprenew]] to collect electronics for recycling or refurbishment. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In Year Five, the partners hosted three events, and each event collected more than 6,000 pounds of electronics. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The city reported that additional events are planned because of the program's early success. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
