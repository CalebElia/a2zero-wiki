The [[initiatives/commercial-ev-charger-program|Commercial EV Charger Program]] is an OSI initiative designed to encourage electric-vehicle adoption by commercial and multifamily property owners in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program offers eligible properties up to four free Level 2 EV chargers, a five-year software package, a five-year warranty, and charger accessories as needed to ensure functionality. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The initiative expands local charging access beyond city-owned sites and implements Strategy 2's EV infrastructure goals. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
