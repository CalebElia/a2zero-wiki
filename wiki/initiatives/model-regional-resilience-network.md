The Ann Arbor Resilience Network is an emerging initiative to formally coordinate [[initiatives/resilience-hubs|Resilience Hubs]] activities across [[locations/washtenaw-county|Washtenaw County]] through a network of community organizations ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). In Year Three, the City of Ann Arbor hosted over a dozen community-based organizations to explore creation of this coordinated resilience network, and it applied for a federal EPA grant to formally launch the network and expand hubs county-wide, building on completion of the second resilience hub at Bryant Neighborhood Community Center ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]).

In Year Four, Ann Arbor reported winning a $1,000,000 U.S. EPA Environmental Justice Government-to-Government grant to start a [[initiatives/model-regional-resilience-network|Model Regional Resilience Network]] and expand [[initiatives/resilience-hubs|Resilience Hubs]] in the city and throughout [[locations/washtenaw-county|Washtenaw County]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The initiative builds on the city's earlier effort to apply for EPA support to formalize a resilience network beyond individual hub sites and connects resilience hub expansion to environmental justice funding and countywide coordination ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). As described in the Year Four report, the network is explicitly regional in scope and tied to broader county-wide climate action ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In the Year 5 priorities, the City identified collaboration with [[actors/washtenaw-county|Washtenaw County]] and other regional stakeholders to expand county-wide climate action and extend many A2ZERO programs across the county, reinforcing the network's regional expansion goals ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).

In Year Five, OSI co-hosted multiple Washtenaw County Resilience Network meetings with the County's own Resilience Office, convening service providers across the county to coordinate resilience efforts ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). However, the $1,000,000 EPA grant funding this initiative was terminated in Year Five; the City is actively disputing the termination ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
