The [[initiatives/sustainable-food-business-coalition|Ann Arbor Area Sustainable Food Business Coalition]] was launched in Year Four to coordinate with the private sector in advancing sustainability across the entirety of Ann Arbor's food system. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The coalition appears in the report as part of Strategy 5's broader materials and circular economy work, linking food businesses to community-wide sustainability efforts. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) Ann Arbor's sustainable food work also includes strategy development around food-system sustainability and business engagement, and the Year 5 priorities identify launch of a sustainable food strategy as a next step. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
