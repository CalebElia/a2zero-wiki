The A2Zero Metrics Dashboard is a public-facing tool designed to transparently track and communicate Ann Arbor's progress toward its climate action goals. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) Launching the dashboard was identified as a Year 3 priority in the A2Zero Year 2 Annual Report. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) OSI launched the A2ZERO Dashboard in year four to show progress on implementing the A2ZERO plan. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The Year 4 report frames the dashboard as part of the cross-cutting engagement, transparency, and metrics-tracking work that supports all A2ZERO strategies. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
