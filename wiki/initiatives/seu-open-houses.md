[[initiatives/seu-open-houses|SEU Open Houses]] is a resident-facing engagement effort piloted by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] to explain the [[initiatives/sustainable-energy-utility|Sustainable Energy Utility]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) In year five, OSI hosted two events in which a host invited neighbors into their homes to learn more about the SEU. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) Based on those sessions, OSI reported that it was developing a template and toolkit others could use to share information about the SEU with friends and neighbors. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
