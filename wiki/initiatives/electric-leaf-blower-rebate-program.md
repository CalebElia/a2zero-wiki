The [[initiatives/electric-leaf-blower-rebate-program|Electric Leaf Blower Rebate Program]] is an OSI rebate initiative designed to lower the cost of switching from gas-powered to electric lawn care equipment in line with Ann Arbor's leaf blower ordinance. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program includes rebates for both residents and landscaping companies to support the transition to electric equipment. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) OSI credited [[actors/grainger-industrial-supply|Grainger Industrial Supply]] and [[actors/weingartz|WEINGARTZ]] with supporting creation of the program. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
