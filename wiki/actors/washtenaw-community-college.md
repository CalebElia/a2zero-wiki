Washtenaw Community College is a two-year public college that partnered with OSI as the host site for a Year Four Emergency Kit Distribution and Household Preparedness Seminar, which distributed 30 emergency kits and developed household preparedness plans with students and staff ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
