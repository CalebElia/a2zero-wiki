Michigan Green Communities is a statewide sustainability recognition program. Ann Arbor received Gold-level recognition first noted in the Year Three annual report and reaffirmed in Year Four ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In Year Five, Ann Arbor became one of two cities to achieve Platinum status, the program's highest level, up from Gold ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
