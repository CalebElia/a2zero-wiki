Michigan Green Communities is a statewide sustainability recognition program. Ann Arbor received Gold-level recognition — the program's highest-level award — first noted in the Year Three annual report and reaffirmed in Year Four ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
