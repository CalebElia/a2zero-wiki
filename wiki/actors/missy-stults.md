Missy Stults (mstults@a2gov.org) is a City of Ann Arbor sustainability staff member responsible for activities supporting Strategy 1 of the A2Zero plan, covering renewable energy grid initiatives ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). She was recognized by GRIST as one of the 50 "Fixers" of 2022 during Year Two of A2Zero ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]).

She was named to Constellation's 50 notable sustainability leaders of 2024 ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]), and remained highly visible into Year Five, including representing OSI at the DTE heating franchise signing alongside Mayor Christopher Taylor and at the Year Five Earth Day celebration with visiting officials from Ann Arbor's sister city Tübingen, Germany ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
