The Michigan Energy Innovation Business Council (MIEIBC) is a state trade association for clean energy businesses. OSI co-hosted a meeting with MIEIBC in February of Year Four to discuss the State of Michigan's rollout of Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) home electrification rebates ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
