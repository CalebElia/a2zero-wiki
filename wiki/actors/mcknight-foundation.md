The McKnight Foundation is a philanthropic organization that awarded $75,000 to support the [[initiatives/bryant-neighborhood-decarbonization|Bryant Neighborhood Decarbonization]] initiative in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). This funding was secured during Year Two of A2Zero (2021–2022) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). The Foundation continued its involvement into Year Five, when OSI and [[actors/community-action-network|Community Action Network]] secured continuing operational support from McKnight for the Bryant neighborhood decarbonization project ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
