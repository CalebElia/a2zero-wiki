AmeriCorps is identified in the A2Zero plan as a partner for coordinating community emergency preparedness and disaster response alongside the Red Cross and other organizations ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

That partnership grew into the [[initiatives/ann-arbor-climate-corps|Ann Arbor Climate Corps]], a national AmeriCorps program supporting education and behavior-change work on A2Zero ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). By Year Five, 14 AmeriCorps members served with the program, 8 completing full terms and together logging 13,600 hours with OSI, earning Segal Education Awards totaling $55,109 ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
