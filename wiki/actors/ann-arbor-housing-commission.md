The [[actors/ann-arbor-housing-commission|Ann Arbor Housing Commission]] is a government body affiliated with the [[actors/city-of-ann-arbor|City of Ann Arbor]] responsible for public housing and managing affordable housing sites in the city ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). The Commission hosted an A2Zero presentation and discussion at its meeting on January 15, 2020, and co-hosted a community outreach event at [[locations/bryant-neighborhood-community-center|Bryant Community Center]] on February 19, 2020, as part of the A2Zero equity engagement effort ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). In Year Two of A2Zero (2021–2022), the Commission worked with the City to secure $2.5 million in federal funding to support energy, comfort, and safety improvements and to transition numerous affordable housing sites to net zero energy, as well as to secure bids for solar installation at multiple sites ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]).

In Year Three, OSI supported geothermal studies and analysis at two future Commission sites and secured $3 million in additional federal aid to advance net zero energy affordable housing ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). The Commission's portfolio continued to expand across Years Four and Five: OSI assisted the Commission in designing pathways to full decarbonization for multiple existing and new buildings, supported installation of solar, geothermal, and electric appliances at multiple sites, and helped the Commission install EV chargers and launch its first EV car-share program ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The Commission's Green Baxter Court community center was selected as one of two next Resilience Hub sites in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]); by Year Five, geothermal had been integrated into the Commission's new affordable housing site on Catherine Street, and a request for proposals was issued for solar and battery storage installation at Green Baxter Court ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
