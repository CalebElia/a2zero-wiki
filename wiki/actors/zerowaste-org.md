[[actors/zerowaste-org|ZeroWaste.Org]] began a three-year contract with the City to promote circular economy activities and engagement throughout the community in Year Four of A2ZERO. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) ZeroWaste.Org also collaborated on Neighborhood Swap Days alongside [[actors/community-action-network|Community Action Network]], Church of the Good Shepherd, and [[actors/ann-arbor-public-schools|Ann Arbor Public Schools]], helping the program reuse more than 6,800 items.
