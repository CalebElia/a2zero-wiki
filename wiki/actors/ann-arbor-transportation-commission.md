A commission within the [[actors/city-of-ann-arbor|City of Ann Arbor]] advising on transportation policy, transit expansion, non-motorized transportation planning, park-and-ride, and tiered parking rates ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). A2Zero's original design lists the Commission as a collaborator on regional transit expansion, alongside the [[actors/ann-arbor-area-transit-authority|Ann Arbor Area Transit Authority]], the Regional Transit Authority of Southeast Michigan, and the Southeast Michigan Council of Governments ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).
