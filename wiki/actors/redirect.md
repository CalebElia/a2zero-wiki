ReDirect is a fellowship program that has supplied sustainability-focused fellows to OSI for at least two consecutive years. In Year Three, ReDirect provided three summer fellows — the second year in a row — to help advance sustainability-focused behavior change activities ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). In Year Four, ReDirect hosted three fellows who developed the framework for the [[initiatives/nourishing-neighborhood-pilot|Nourishing Neighborhood Pilot]] in coordination with OSI and the [[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Office of Emergency Management]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
