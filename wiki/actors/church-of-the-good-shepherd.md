Church of the Good Shepherd is a local Ann Arbor congregation that partnered with OSI, [[actors/community-action-network|Community Action Network]], [[actors/ann-arbor-public-schools|Ann Arbor Public Schools]], and [[actors/zerowaste-org|ZeroWaste.Org]] to continue hosting Neighborhood Swap Days in Year Four, together reusing more than 6,800 items through the program ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
