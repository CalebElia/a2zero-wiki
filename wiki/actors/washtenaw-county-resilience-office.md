The [[actors/washtenaw-county-resilience-office|Washtenaw County Resilience Office]] is a county government office that partnered with [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] to co-host multiple [[initiatives/washtenaw-county-resilience-network|Washtenaw County Resilience Network]] meetings in Year Five. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The office's role in this section is to help convene critical service providers across Washtenaw County to coordinate resilience initiatives and collaborative climate resilience work. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
