Christopher Taylor served as Mayor of Ann Arbor and was the lead sponsor of the November 4, 2019 City Council resolution declaring a climate emergency and directing the creation of the 2030 Carbon Neutral Ann Arbor Plan ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). Taylor appears as Mayor in Year Five sources through May 2025, including the unveiling of the state's first curbside EV chargers and the DTE heating franchise signing ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
