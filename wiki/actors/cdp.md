CDP (formerly the Carbon Disclosure Project) is a global environmental disclosure nonprofit that recognized Ann Arbor as one of 119 global climate action leaders on its 2023 A List, a recognition first noted in the Year Three annual report and reaffirmed in Year Four's discussion of institutional credibility and regional learning ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
