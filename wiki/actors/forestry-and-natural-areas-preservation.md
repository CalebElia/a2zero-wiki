Forestry and Natural Areas Preservation (NAP) is a City of Ann Arbor unit responsible for the urban tree canopy and natural area stewardship. It partnered with OSI and Public Works on the Year Three [[actors/us-forest-service|USFS]] Urban & Community Forestry grant proposal ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]), and in Year Four the city won a $1,000,000 USFS grant in conjunction with NAP for a comprehensive tree canopy analysis, tree planting and maintenance in underserved neighborhoods, and stewardship work in natural areas under the [[initiatives/10000-trees-initiative|10,000 Trees Initiative]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
