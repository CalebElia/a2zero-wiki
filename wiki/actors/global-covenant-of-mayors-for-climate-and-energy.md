The Global Covenant of Mayors for Climate and Energy is an international coalition of local governments committed to climate action. It awarded Ann Arbor mitigation and adaptation badges in Year Four, recognizing the city's institutional credibility in both reducing emissions and building climate resilience ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
