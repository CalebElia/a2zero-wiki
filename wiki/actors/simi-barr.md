Simi Barr is a City of Ann Arbor sustainability staff member who was recognized by Crain's as an electric vehicle champion during Year Two of A2Zero (2021–2022) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]).

By Year Four, Barr held the title of Senior Analyst and appeared alongside Mayor Christopher Taylor at the unveiling of the State of Michigan's first curbside pole-mounted EV chargers ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
