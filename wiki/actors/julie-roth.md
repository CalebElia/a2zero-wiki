Julie Roth is a City of Ann Arbor sustainability staff member who was awarded the George Sexton Public Servant of the Earth Award from the Sierra Club during Year Two of A2Zero (2021–2022) ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]).

She was named a Crain's Detroit 2023 Notable Leader in Energy ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) and continued as a visible OSI representative into Year Five, including speaking at the 2025 Climate Teach-In ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
