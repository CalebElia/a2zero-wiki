The Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) is a federal agency within the [[actors/us-federal-government|United States Federal Government]]. In Year Four, OSI and the [[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Office of Emergency Management]] submitted a FEMA Building Resilient Infrastructure and Communities (BRIC) grant application to install a microgrid at [[locations/fire-station-3|Fire Station 3]] and [[locations/veterans-memorial-park|Veterans Memorial Park]], part of the city's broader resilience investment in backup power capacity ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
