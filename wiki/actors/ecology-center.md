The Ecology Center is listed as a project co-designer for the [[initiatives/green-business-challenge|Green Business Challenge]], contributing expertise in waste reduction and environmental sustainability for local businesses ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The organization is one of a broad coalition of partners supporting the challenge alongside utilities, financial institutions, and business associations ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

In Year Three, the City began working with The Ecology Center to improve resource management across five areas: youth engagement (2nd and 7th grade field trips), multi-family units, commercial businesses, events outreach, and design of a social-media-based marketing campaign ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]).
