The Leslie Science and Nature Center is one of the 67 organizations that signed on as an original A2Zero partner by early March 2020 ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). It partnered with OSI to host an annual Earth Day celebration as part of A2Zero's Year Four communications and engagement work, alongside the Green Fair, A2ZERO weeks, and the Sustainability Forum series ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]); this Earth Day partnership recurred in Year Five ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). In Year Five, the Center also collaborated with OSI and [[actors/neutral-zone|Neutral Zone]] to create Youth Now Climate Action, a group of Ann Arbor Public Schools high school students focused on identifying environmental problems and developing solutions ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
