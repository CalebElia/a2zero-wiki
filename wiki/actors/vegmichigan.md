[[actors/vegmichigan|VegMichigan]] began a three-year contract with the City in Year Four to promote and engage the community and local businesses around plant-forward diets. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) In the first three quarters of the contract, VegMichigan held more than 50 public events with upwards of 2,600 total attendees, enrolled 529 residents in its 30-Day Plant-Based Pledge program, and reported that 93% of follow-up survey respondents said they would continue eating a more plant-based diet after the pledge period. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) VegMichigan also engaged 15 local businesses offering plant-based options and worked with one local organization to implement a plant-based purchasing policy. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
