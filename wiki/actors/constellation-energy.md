[[actors/constellation-energy|Constellation Energy]] is identified as the City of Ann Arbor's alternative energy supplier under Michigan's Electric Customer Choice Program. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report states that, through collaboration with Constellation Energy and enrollment of 67 additional city sites in the program, more than 70% of the electricity used to power city facilities and operations is now generated by renewable energy sources. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report also describes Constellation as the nation's largest producer of carbon-free energy. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
