The senior appointed executive within the [[actors/city-of-ann-arbor|City of Ann Arbor]] responsible for day-to-day operations. Referenced in A2Zero's internal carbon price and equity programs work.

In June 2017, predating A2Zero, City Council approved Resolution R-17-237 directing the City Administrator to revive and update the City's Green Fleets Policy, promoting the purchase of plug-in electric vehicles and incorporating best practices from peer municipalities — the origin of the [[initiatives/electrify-city-fleet|Electrify City Fleet]] initiative ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The November 2019 climate emergency resolution directed the City Administrator to design the community engagement process for A2Zero, facilitate collaboration with the University of Michigan's President's Commission on Carbon Neutrality, and factor the plan's likely outcomes into FY21 budget planning ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

As of Year Five, the position was held by City Administrator Dohoney ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]); the role has been held by multiple people across A2Zero's history and this page tracks the office, not a specific individual.
