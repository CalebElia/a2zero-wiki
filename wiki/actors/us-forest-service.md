The U.S. Forest Service (USFS) is the federal agency that funds Ann Arbor's urban forestry work under the [[initiatives/10000-trees-initiative|10,000 Trees Initiative]]. In Year Three, OSI submitted a USDA Forest Service Urban & Community Forestry Inflation Reduction Act grant proposal, together with Public Works and [[actors/forestry-and-natural-areas-preservation|Forestry and Natural Areas Preservation]], to fund an update to Ann Arbor's Urban Tree Canopy assessment, advance private property tree planting, create a Natural Areas Preservation management plan, and conduct a feasibility study for a City- and County-owned tree nursery ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). In Year Four, the city reported winning a $1,000,000 grant from the USFS, in conjunction with Forestry and Natural Areas Preservation, for a comprehensive tree canopy analysis, tree planting and maintenance in underserved neighborhoods, and stewardship work in natural areas ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
