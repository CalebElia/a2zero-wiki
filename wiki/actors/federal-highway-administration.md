The Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) is a federal agency within the [[actors/us-federal-government|United States Federal Government]] that funds and oversees highway and surface-transportation infrastructure. In Year Five, [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] won a $2.8 million Charging and Fueling Infrastructure (CFI) grant from FHWA to expand public EV charging in Ann Arbor, funding the [[funding-events/ann-arbor-cfi-grant-2025|Ann Arbor CFI Grant 2025]] and supporting the [[initiatives/expand-ev-charging-infrastructure|Expand EV Charging Infrastructure]] initiative; hiring for an EV coordinator to administer the four-year award was underway as of the report ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
