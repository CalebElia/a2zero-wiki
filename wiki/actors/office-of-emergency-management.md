[[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Office of Emergency Management]], also referred to as [[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Ann Arbor Emergency Management]], is a City of Ann Arbor government office responsible for emergency preparedness and hazard mitigation. It operates under the [[actors/ann-arbor-fire-department|Ann Arbor Fire Department]] within the [[actors/city-of-ann-arbor|City of Ann Arbor]]'s government structure. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) In Year Two of A2Zero, the office worked with the A2Zero team to integrate climate change and resilience projects into the City's updated multi-hazard mitigation plan, and supported the staffing of an emergency preparedness shelter during a 5-day power outage in August 2021. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]) In year four, it partnered with OSI on an elections tabletop exercise for winter preparedness and on a FEMA BRIC grant application for a microgrid at Fire Station 3 and Veterans Memorial Park. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) Its role in the report is as a city government partner for emergency planning and resilience infrastructure. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])

In Year Five, the office partnered with OSI on two household preparedness seminars — one at Washtenaw Community College distributing 30 emergency kits, and a second at [[actors/avalon-housing|Avalon Housing]] distributing 20 more — with plans to scale the workshop model to additional community partners ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
