Bicentennial Park is a City of Ann Arbor park. By Year Four, active design work was underway for nearly 4 MW of additional solar at Bicentennial Park and several other city sites, as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment, with construction expected in Year Five ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). However, by Year Five this installation had not yet occurred; the Year Five report re-listed Bicentennial Park among the city facilities where solar has been newly designed and is now planned for installation in Year Six ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
