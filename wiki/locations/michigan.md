The U.S. state containing Ann Arbor. Regulatory and geographic context for A2Zero's grant relationships with [[actors/michigan-egle|EGLE]] and [[actors/michigan-department-of-transportation|MDOT]].

Michigan's electricity grid is undergoing a state-mandated transition to clean energy under SB 271 (see [[actors/state-of-michigan]]), shaping the fuel mix available to communities across the state, including Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).

In early 2025, the state adopted updated Michigan building codes, though the updates do not yet apply to the Residential Code ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). OSI intervened for the first time in two gas cases before the [[actors/michigan-public-service-commission|Michigan Public Service Commission]], calling for a "Future of Gas" proceeding outlining a path to decarbonized heating and cooling throughout DTE's service territory, and participated in multiple additional Commission working groups and technical conferences ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). Ann Arbor became one of two cities statewide to achieve Platinum status in the Michigan Green Communities program that year ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
