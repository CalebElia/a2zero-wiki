Bandemer Park is a City of Ann Arbor natural area park. In Year Three, the City continued its adoption of Bandemer Park through GIVE365's Adopt-A-Park program ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). The city continued adoption of Bandemer Park in Year Four, holding monthly volunteer workdays focused on litter cleanup, native planting, invasive species removal, and general facility maintenance, part of the broader natural systems and stewardship work under the [[initiatives/10000-trees-initiative|10,000 Trees Initiative]] ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
