[[locations/fire-station-3|Fire Station 3]] is identified in Year Four as a city facility included in renewable-energy and resilience infrastructure planning. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The report says the city was designing additional solar for the site and had received a $70,000 grant to design a microgrid at Fire Station 3 together with [[locations/veterans-memorial-park|Veterans Memorial Park]], while also submitting a federal FEMA BRIC grant application through OSI and Emergency Management to finalize the design and install the microgrid. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])
