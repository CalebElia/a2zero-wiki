Buhr Park is a City of Ann Arbor park. In Year Four, the city installed over 250 kW of rooftop and ground-mounted solar across several city sites including Buhr Park, as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
