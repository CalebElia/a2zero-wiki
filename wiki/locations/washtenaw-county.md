Washtenaw County is the county jurisdiction containing Ann Arbor and Ypsilanti, located in southeast Michigan. Several A2ZERO programs extend to county-wide partnerships and beneficiaries, particularly for community solar, weatherization, and transit initiatives ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

By Year 5, 711 Solarize bulk-buy solar installations had been recorded across Washtenaw County, extending beyond the program's original Ann Arbor footprint ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). The [[actors/ann-arbor-washtenaw-2030-district|Ann Arbor/Washtenaw 2030 District]], a regional coalition supporting commercial, multifamily, and non-profit building efficiency, spans the county ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]), and a Commercial Solar Program launched in partnership with the 2030 District incentivizes commercial solar adoption county-wide ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]).
