The [[locations/ann-arbor-senior-center|Ann Arbor Senior Center]] is a City of Ann Arbor facility designated as the 3rd planned Resilience Hub under the A2Zero Resilience Hubs initiative, with design completed in Year Two and implementation planned for Year 3 ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]]). In Year Four, the City installed a 250kW solar array at the Senior Center, one of several municipal sites to receive solar installations that year, along with the addition of battery storage backup at the facility to support its role as a Resilience Hub ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
