Fuller Park is a City of Ann Arbor park. In Year Four, the city installed over 250 kW of rooftop and ground-mounted solar across several city sites including Fuller Park, as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In Year Five, the City reported 700 kW of new solar systems installed on City properties, including at Fuller Park, Veterans Memorial Park, and the Ann Arbor airport, bringing total solar generating capacity on City properties above 1 MW ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
