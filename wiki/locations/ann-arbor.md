Ann Arbor, Michigan is the geographic scope of the [[plans/a2zero-carbon-neutrality-plan|A2Zero]] carbon neutrality initiative, with a community-wide greenhouse gas emissions baseline of 2.1 million metric tons as of 2018 ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). The city faces documented climate impacts including a nearly 1°F increase in average annual temperature since the 1900s, a projected 3–7°F additional rise by 2050, and a 44% increase in average annual precipitation since the 1950s ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]). Extreme precipitation events have also intensified, with total rainfall during such events increasing 37% since 1981, and Ann Arbor is projected to experience 12–36 more days per year above 90°F by 2050 ([[sources/cap/cap-2020|cap-2020]]).

Community-wide greenhouse gas emissions declined roughly 22% relative to the 2018 baseline by 2022 ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]), and by 2024 the City reported a milestone of having stopped community emissions from increasing further, ahead of the harder work of reducing existing emissions ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). Electricity use in homes and businesses is consistently the largest source of local emissions, at 39–41% of the total across Years 3–5, followed by fossil gas used in buildings at roughly 28% ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). The carbon intensity of electricity serving Ann Arbor has been improving: DTE's fuel mix supplying the community was 54% coal-generated in 2022, falling to 40% by 2024, with renewables holding around 13% over that period ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).

Ann Arbor has been recognized as a Gold-level "Walk Friendly" community and a Gold-level Bicycle Friendly Community by the League of American Bicyclists in multiple consecutive years ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]], [[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
