In Year One, OSI staff delivered a keynote speech at the Washtenaw Community College Climate Summit ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year1|a2zero-year1]]). Washtenaw Community College hosted an Emergency Kit Distribution and Household Preparedness Seminar organized by OSI in year four. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) At that event, 30 emergency kits were distributed and household preparedness plans were developed with students and staff. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The Year Five report also describes this seminar, delivered in partnership with the [[actors/office-of-emergency-management|Office of Emergency Management]], noting that plans are underway to scale the workshop model to more community partners ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
