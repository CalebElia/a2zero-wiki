[[locations/peace-neighborhood-center|Peace Neighborhood Center]] was identified in Year Four as one of the next two [[initiatives/resilience-hubs|Resilience Hub]] sites in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The report states that initial engagement and planning for the site were underway as part of the city's resilience hub expansion effort ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). In Year Five, the Peace Neighborhood Center Resilience Hub scheduled a LED lighting retrofit and HVAC control installation to improve energy efficiency and allow savings to be reinvested into its programs, and OSI supported the center in preparing for future on-site solar and energy storage to help manage energy-related disruptions ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]). OSI also assisted Peace Neighborhood Center in integrating sustainability into its STEAM summer programming curriculum ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]).
