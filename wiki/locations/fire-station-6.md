Fire Station 6 is a City of Ann Arbor fire station. By Year Four, active design work was underway for nearly 4 MW of additional solar at Fire Station 6 and several other city sites, as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment, with construction expected in Year Five ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
