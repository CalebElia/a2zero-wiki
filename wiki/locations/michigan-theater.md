[[locations/michigan-theater|Michigan Theater]] was the venue for the first [[initiatives/cinema-and-sustainability-film-series|Cinema and Sustainability Film Series]] hosted by [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] in 2024. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The series screened four documentaries with post-film panel discussions and drew more than 250 attendees across all screenings.
