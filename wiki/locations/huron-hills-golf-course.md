Huron Hills Golf Course is a City of Ann Arbor municipal golf course. In Year Four, the city installed over 250 kW of rooftop and ground-mounted solar across several city sites including Huron Hills Golf Course, as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
