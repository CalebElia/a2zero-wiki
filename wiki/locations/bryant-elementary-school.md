Bryant Elementary School is a public elementary school in Ann Arbor's Bryant neighborhood. In Year Four, the district geothermal system moving into design through a DOE grant was scoped to cover at least 75% of the heating and cooling load for 262 households, the county mental health facility, Bryant Elementary School, the [[locations/bryant-neighborhood-community-center|Bryant Community Center]], and the city's public works facility ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
