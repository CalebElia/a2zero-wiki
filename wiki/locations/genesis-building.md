The Genesis Building is an Ann Arbor community facility. In Year Four, the A2ZERO Ambassador team and COSA (Community Organization Sustainability Advisors) assisted the Genesis Building with the construction of a rain garden to help address flooding issues on the property ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
