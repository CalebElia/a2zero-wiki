The [[locations/green-baxter-court-community-center|Green Baxter Court community center]] was named in Year Four as one of the next two planned [[initiatives/resilience-hubs|Resilience Hub]] sites in Ann Arbor ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The report identifies the site as Ann Arbor Housing Commission's Green Baxter Court community center and says initial engagement and planning were underway ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
