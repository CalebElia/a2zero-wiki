Wheeler Service Center is a City of Ann Arbor public works facility. It was one of the initial municipal sites selected for the roughly 3 MW solar installation contract with vendors Enerlogics and Melink Solar, with construction beginning fall 2023 ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]); a $25,000 State of Michigan grant supported an LED lighting conversion there in Year Three ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]). By Year Four, an LED retrofit was completed at Wheeler Service Center, and it was also identified as a site for nearly 4 MW of additional solar under active design as part of the [[initiatives/arpa-city-facilities-solar|ARPA City Facilities Solar]] deployment, with construction expected in Year Five ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
