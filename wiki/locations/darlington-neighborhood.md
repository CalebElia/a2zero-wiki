The Darlington Neighborhood is an Ann Arbor residential neighborhood. It was chosen as the pilot site for the [[initiatives/nourishing-neighborhood-pilot|Nourishing Neighborhood Pilot]] in Year Four, hosting the initial neighborhood gathering led by A2ZERO Ambassadors and a second pilot event focused on residents' experiences with winter power outages and emergency preparedness ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
