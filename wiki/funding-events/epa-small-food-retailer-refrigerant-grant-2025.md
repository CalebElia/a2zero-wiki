OSI received grant funding from the [[actors/us-epa|EPA]] for a small food retailer refrigerant management program. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The program targets at least 75 businesses and aims to reduce refrigeration emissions by a total of 13,074.9 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report does not state the dollar amount of the grant award. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
