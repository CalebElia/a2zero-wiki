In Year Five, [[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] won a $2.8 million Charging and Fueling Infrastructure grant from the [[actors/federal-highway-administration|Federal Highway Administration]] to expand public EV charging in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report states that hiring was underway for an electric vehicle coordinator to administer this work over the next four years. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) This funding event supports Ann Arbor's broader EV charging expansion efforts. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
