[[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] supported [[actors/ann-arbor-area-transit-authority|TheRide]] in a successful $25 million federal grant application to fund zero-emission buses and refueling stations. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The funding is part of the Federal Transit Administration's No Emission Bus Grant program. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) This award funds the [[initiatives/electrify-buses|Electrify Buses]] initiative by advancing transit vehicle electrification and associated fueling infrastructure.
