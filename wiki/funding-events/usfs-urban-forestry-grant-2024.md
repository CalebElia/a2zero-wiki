Ann Arbor reported winning a $1,000,000 grant from the [[actors/usda-forest-service|USFS]] in conjunction with Forestry and Natural Areas Preservation in Year Four ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]). The grant supports a comprehensive tree canopy analysis, tree planting and maintenance in underserved neighborhoods, and critical stewardship work in natural areas through the [[initiatives/10000-trees-initiative|10,000 Trees Initiative]] context ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]).
