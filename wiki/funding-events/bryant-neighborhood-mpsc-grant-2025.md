[[actors/office-of-sustainability-and-innovations|OSI]] secured a $3.1 million grant from the [[actors/michigan-public-service-commission|Michigan Public Service Commission]] to improve energy efficiency and electrify homes in the Bryant neighborhood. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report states that foundational work is underway so the program can begin making improvements in year six. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The funding supports neighborhood decarbonization through household efficiency and electrification upgrades.
