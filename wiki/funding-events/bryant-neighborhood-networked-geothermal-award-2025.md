OSI and a team of collaborators reported winning $10,000,000 in 2025 from the [[actors/us-department-of-energy|Department of Energy]] to support deployment and begin installation of a networked district geothermal system in the Bryant neighborhood of Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The award funds implementation work under the [[initiatives/geothermal-districts|Geothermal Districts]] initiative. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report states that this funding is currently on hold. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
