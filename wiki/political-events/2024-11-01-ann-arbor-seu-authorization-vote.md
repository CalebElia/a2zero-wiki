In November 2024, Ann Arbor voters overwhelmingly voted to authorize creation of the [[initiatives/sustainable-energy-utility|Sustainable Energy Utility]]. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The vote gates the formal creation of the SEU and marks a major milestone in Ann Arbor's local renewable-energy transition. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The annual report presents this authorization alongside state clean-energy legislation and local geothermal deployment as part of a broader shift in Michigan's clean-energy landscape. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
