On April 22, 2025, Ann Arbor and [[actors/dte-energy|DTE Energy]] signed a heating franchise that the report describes as the first in the country to include climate commitments and dedicated resources to address the climate crisis. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The franchise includes an Agreement Regarding Climate Action that outlines commitments to cooperate in support of A2ZERO and DTE's climate goals. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) A two-year work plan also specifies actions that Ann Arbor and DTE will take to advance climate action. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
