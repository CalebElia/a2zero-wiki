The city reported receiving a letter terminating its $1,000,000 U.S. EPA Environmental Justice Government-to-Government grant intended to start a regional resilience network and expand resilience hubs in Ann Arbor and Washtenaw County. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The city also reported that it is actively disputing this termination. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
