Year Four flagged that Ann Arbor voters would have the opportunity to decide whether the City should formally create a Sustainable Energy Utility by voting in the November 2024 election, describing the SEU as an optional, supplemental, and community-owned utility authorized to deploy renewable energy in Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])

On November 5, 2024, Ann Arbor voters authorized creation of the [[initiatives/sustainable-energy-utility|Sustainable Energy Utility]] with 79% of the vote. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The report describes the SEU as an opt-in, supplemental utility that will provide clean, 100% renewable power to residents of the City of Ann Arbor. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) This vote gates formal launch of the SEU and was followed immediately by startup work and public waitlist enrollment. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]])
