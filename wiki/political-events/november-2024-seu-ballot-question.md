Year Four states that Ann Arbor voters will have the opportunity to determine whether the City should formally create a Sustainable Energy Utility by voting in the November 2024 election. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]]) The report describes the SEU as an optional, supplemental, and community-owned utility authorized to deploy renewable energy in Ann Arbor.
