[[actors/dte-energy|DTE Energy]] remains the key external actor because utility interconnection, landfill solar development, and broader grid conditions shape what the city can actually deliver. The year-over-year arc suggests increasing diversification rather than convergence on a single delivery model, with major open questions still centered on state authorization, utility partnership, and scaling local generation fast enough to matter by 2030.

[[strategies/strategy-2-electrification|Strategy 2: Building Electrification]] has moved from a six-action planning framework toward a more implementation-oriented mix of building, fleet, and charging efforts. The portfolio now spans [[initiatives/home-and-business-electrification|home and business electrification]], [[initiatives/electrify-city-fleet|city fleet electrification]], [[initiatives/electrify-private-fleets|private fleet electrification]], [[initiatives/electrify-buses|bus electrification]], [[initiatives/expand-ev-charging-infrastructure|EV charging expansion]], and enabling policies like the [[initiatives/ev-readiness-ordinance|EV-readiness ordinance]]. A notable feature of the arc is that electrification increasingly depends on upstream infrastructure, especially [[initiatives/grid-capacity-expansion-electrification|grid capacity expansion for electrification]], rather than only on end-user adoption. By year 4, the strategy reads less like a standalone technology shift and more like a systems transition whose pace is constrained by utility coordination, charging deployment, and measurable uptake in homes, businesses, and fleets.

[[strategies/strategy-3-building-efficiency|Strategy 3: Building Energy Efficiency]] remains one of the broadest and most implementation-rich strategies, growing from an 11-action design into a layered portfolio of policy, financing, and direct support tools. The millage in particular appears to be a pivotal implementation actor in practice, because it likely underwrites rebates, grants, and equity-centered delivery across multiple strategies. By year 4, the strategy’s importance is clear even though its measurable outputs remain less standardized in the wiki than its role as the civic, financial, and equity connective tissue of the broader plan.
