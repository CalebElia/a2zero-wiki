The [[technology/sesame-solar-unit|Sesame Solar Unit]] is a mobile nanogrid that Ann Arbor uses for sustainability-related education and as a source of backup power for central city facilities. ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) Year Five reports that the unit is coming to the Ann Arbor community and is expected to appear around the city during the summer and fall.
