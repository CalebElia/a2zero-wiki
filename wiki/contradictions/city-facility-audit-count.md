## Conflicting claims

**Year 1:** "Completed energy audits of 6 City facilities" — a bare Strategy 3 bullet, no buildings named, no mention of decarbonization planning or a specific completion goal ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year1|a2zero-year1]]).

**Year 2:** "Conducted energy audits of 4 City facilities and developing a plan to fully decarbonize these buildings." ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year2|a2zero-year2]])

**Year 3:** "Began decarbonization audits with a local engineering team at four municipal buildings, with the goal of fully decarbonizing these buildings by 2025." ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year3|a2zero-year3]]) This is the report that anchors [[initiatives/municipal-building-decarbonization-audits|the Municipal Building Decarbonization Audits initiative page]] — Year 2's "4 facilities... developing a plan to decarbonize" and Year 3's "four municipal buildings... goal of fully decarbonizing by 2025" clearly describe the same continuing effort.

**Year 4:** "Conducted decarbonization assessments at six municipal facilities and developed plans to make these carbon neutral buildings. Also initiated work to support decarbonization of the City's new election center" (mentioned separately, implying the election center is not one of the six). ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year4|a2zero-year4]])

**Year 5:** Makes no mention of a facility count for this effort at all — no confirmation, no update, no building names anywhere in the report.

## The ambiguity

No source ever names the individual buildings, so none of the four counts can be cross-referenced directly. Three open questions:

1. **Is Year 1's "6" the same effort as Years 2-4's decarbonization-specific track, or a separate, broader baseline energy-audit sweep** that predates the decarbonization framing entirely? Year 1's bullet doesn't mention decarbonization or a completion target the way Years 2-4 do — it reads as a more generic Strategy 3 efficiency action alongside benchmarking-ordinance drafting and code-committee work.
2. **If it is the same effort, does Year 2/3's "4" represent a subset of Year 1's "6"** (six audited, four selected for full decarbonization planning), or a fresh, unrelated set of four audits?
3. **Does Year 4's "6" represent growth from the Year 2/3 four (two buildings added), a return to Year 1's original six, or a cumulative total** that includes buildings from multiple prior years?

## Best-guess explanation

The Year 2 → Year 3 continuity is solid: "4 facilities... developing a plan to decarbonize" (Year 2) directly precedes "four municipal buildings... goal of fully decarbonizing by 2025" (Year 3) — same count, same framing, one year apart. That pairing is the initiative's real anchor.

Year 1's six is the least certain data point. Its phrasing ("energy audits," no decarbonization language, no completion goal) is different enough from the Year 2-4 pattern that it may describe a distinct, earlier baseline-scan activity rather than this initiative's origin — but the Strategy 3 section and general subject (city-facility energy audits) are close enough that a shared or overlapping building set can't be ruled out either.

Year 4's return to six is the hardest to place. Its language ("decarbonization assessments," "plans to make... carbon neutral") matches the Year 2/3 track's framing exactly, which argues against it being a coincidental echo of Year 1's unrelated six. The most plausible reading is that the four-building pilot expanded by two buildings between Year 3 and Year 4 — but nothing in the source record confirms this over the alternative that Year 4 restarted counting from a different baseline.

## Why it matters

A replication consultant reading this initiative's page needs to know whether "the decarbonization pilot" is a stable four-building program or one that has already grown to six — that's the difference between citing a contained pilot and citing an initiative already scaling past its original scope. Until a source names the actual buildings, any claim that ties a specific count to "the" municipal decarbonization program is picking one of at least three plausible readings without saying so. Watch Year 6 (or later) for either a named building list or an explicit statement of cumulative scope.
