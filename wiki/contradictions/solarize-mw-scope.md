## Conflicting figures

The Year 5 report states three different solar MW figures that are not reconciled against each other anywhere in the source:

**5.4MW** — "Our Solarize program reached 5.4MW of solar installed through the program!" ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) The narrowest scope: residential solar installed through the [[initiatives/solarize-ann-arbor|Solarize Ann Arbor]] bulk-buy program specifically.

**6.5MW** — "Over 6.5 MW of solar has been installed in Ann Arbor since the adoption of the A2ZERO Plan." ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) Broader than Solarize alone — the Year 5 report's own [[initiatives/solarize-ann-arbor|Solarize Ann Arbor]] narrative separately mentions city-facility and non-Solarize installations that this figure likely includes.

**11.88MW** — "The dashboard reports 11.88 MW of new solar capacity installed." ([[sources/annual-reports/a2zero-year5|a2zero-year5]]) More than double the 5.4MW figure and 5+ MW above the 6.5MW figure — its scope (Ann Arbor only? University of Michigan campus included? a different measurement boundary or time window?) is not stated.

## Why it matters

[[strategies/strategy-1-renewable-grid|Strategy 1's]] long-term target is 78 MW of local renewable generation by 2030. The three figures put current progress at meaningfully different points against that target:
- At 5.4-6.5MW: roughly 7-8% of the 78MW goal.
- At 11.88MW: roughly 15% of the 78MW goal — more than double the lower estimate.

This is not a rounding difference. Whichever figure a reader or downstream consultant cites as "current solar progress" produces a materially different read on whether the program is on track, and the report itself gives no basis for choosing between them.

## Best-guess explanation

- 5.4MW is almost certainly Solarize-program-only, residential bulk-buy installations.
- 6.5MW plausibly adds city-facility solar and other Ann Arbor-based commercial/institutional installations the Solarize figure excludes.
- 11.88MW is unexplained by anything else in the source — it may include University of Michigan campus installations, a broader geographic boundary (Washtenaw County rather than city limits, consistent with the Solarize page's own note that Year 5 "711 Solarize installations" were "reported in Washtenaw County"), or a different measurement window than the other two figures.

None of these are confirmed. Resolution requires the A2ZERO dashboard's own stated methodology and geographic/entity boundary, which is not included in the annual report narrative.
